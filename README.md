DB's Rejuvenating Settings — a mod for Pokémon Rejuvenation V14 (partially tested on 14.0.24)


What it does:
- A second starter: Eternal Flower Floette.
- Legendaries, mythicals, Ultra Beasts and Paradox Pokémon that V14 never gives you
  can be unlocked. Each one needs a condition from its own story, a minimum number
  of badges (matched to the base game's pace) and 1 ticket.
- Tickets come from badges, Chapter Trials, Rift bosses, the Virtual League and the
  Ultimate Trial. There are far fewer tickets than legendaries, so you have to choose.
- Missed legendaries: a second chance for legendaries the game gives, if you missed
  them for good. You can never get the same one twice.
- Trials: Chapter Trials (you fight a copy of your own team), Guardian Trials (real
  competitive teams guarding each "box" legendary) and an end-game Ultimate Trial.
- Bond system (5 tiers): new legendaries only obey you fully once they trust you.
  Higher tiers unlock Mega Stones, a nature change and an ability change.
- Team budget: the mod's legendaries cost points, and your budget grows with the story.
- Balance without extra restrictions: in Normal mode, enemy trainers get a few extra
  items when you use the mod's Pokémon (3 at most, 5 for bosses; no item spam).
- Candy shop: Rare and Reverse Candy with rising prices, reset at each chapter or
  badge. The level cap is never broken.
- Missing Mega/Giga Stones, Z-Crystals and form items, plus a few data fixes.
- Every rule is explained in game: Pause > DB Rejuv Settings > Guide.
- It never touches base game files and never blocks the story.

Install: copy the "patch" folder into your game folder (merge). Full details are in
DB_Rejuvenating_README.txt.
